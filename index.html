1) Project Description

What it is & Why it exists [cite: 14-15, 22]

Odds Against Life is a digital comic book aimed at preventing gambling addiction[cite: 16]. It solves the problem of financial and emotional ruin caused by casinos[cite: 23].

Unique Solution [cite: 17, 25]

Unlike dry statistics, our comic uses empathy to show the decay of addiction, acting as a "visual shield"[cite: 18, 25]. This problem is vital because it affects millions of families[cite: 24].
