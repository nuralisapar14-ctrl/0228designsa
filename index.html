1) Mission & Motivation

The "Why" Behind the Project

Our primary motivation is to showcase **team synergy** and **artistic mastery** while addressing a critical social crisis. We believe that traditional lectures fail to reach youth; therefore, we use the visual power of Manga to create a "mental vaccine" against ludomania.

Our Goal

To provide a relatable, high-stakes narrative that helps teenagers visualize the long-term consequences of gambling addiction and empowers them to make the right choice.

2, 3 & 4) Functional Prototype

Strategic Implementation

  • Bifurcation Point: The final panels feature a dual-ending logic, forcing psychological reflection.

5) Stakeholder Consultation

Insights from Almas (Project Sponsor)

Q: Why is a Manga-style comic better than a poster?

Almas: "Engagement. Teens are desensitized to warnings, but they are drawn to narratives. A 16-page arc allows them to live through the character's mistakes without losing anything in real life."

Q: Does the lack of color reduce the impact?

Almas: "On the contrary. The B&W style removes the 'glamour' of gambling. It focuses purely on the character's facial expressions and the psychological weight of their situation."

Q: What is the most vital part of the story?

Almas: "The moment of realization. Your focus on the 'Good' vs 'Bad' ending contrast is a powerful tool for self-reflection."

Pivot Points

Based on this feedback, we focused on Close-up Emotional Shots and a High-Contrast Inking technique to make the "trap" of gambling feel visually suffocating.
