1) Project Description

The Mission

Odds Against Life is an interactive digital comic created to prevent ludomania among teenagers.

We use visual storytelling to bridge the gap between dry statistics and real emotional consequences. Our goal is to create a "mental vaccine" against the lure of quick money.

Why it Works

Unlike traditional posters, our 16-page narrative immerses the reader in the protagonist's life, making the final choice feel personal and earned.

2) & 4) Functionality & Prototype

Interactive Mechanics

The core feature is the Seamless Vertical Scroll. It mimics the "Webtoon" style popular among modern readers.

  • Format: 16 digital panels in A5 format.
  • Platform: Web-based mobile-responsive reader.
  • Logic: Contrast-based parallel reality reveal.

5) Stakeholder Interview

Interview with Almas (Teacher/Sponsor)

Q: Is gambling a serious national issue?

Almas: "Yes. Government measures and strict age limits prove it's a growing threat to youth stability."

Q: Should we focus on emotions or hard facts?

Almas: "Emotions. If readers can feel the stress and regret of the character, the message will be far more effective than just numbers."

Q: What makes a powerful ending?

Almas: "A moment of realization. Showing that there is always a turning point gives the reader hope and clarity."

Key Project Adjustments

Based on the interview, we implemented:

  • Visual Focus: More close-up emotional shots to build empathy.
  • End-Goal: The "Good Ending" now focuses on family and freedom, not just financial gain.
  • Readability: Finalized A5 vertical format for better mobile UX.
