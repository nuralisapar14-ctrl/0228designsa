1) Project Description

What it is & Why it exists

Odds Against Life is an immersive digital comic book designed to educate and warn about the devastating effects of casino addiction.

The Problem: Addiction leads to financial ruin and loss of life. We aim to prevent this through art.

The Solution & Uniqueness

The Solution: The comic creates an emotional deterrent, showing the raw reality of addiction before people start gambling.

Uniqueness: We use engaging visual storytelling instead of boring lectures to reach young audiences.
