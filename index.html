Breaking the Cycle of Addiction

A visual journey through the dangers of gambling and the path to recovery.

Learn More

Project Description

What it is: An immersive digital comic designed to educate and warn about the devastating effects of casino addiction[cite: 4].

The Problem: Over 60 million people worldwide suffer from gambling addiction. It is a silent killer that leads to financial ruin and loss of life[cite: 4].

Why it Matters: This problem is critical because it destroys families and health. Our comic aims to prevent these deaths by providing a relatable narrative for all ages[cite: 4].

Uniqueness: Unlike clinical brochures, our comic uses visual storytelling and psychological triggers to help readers "feel" the consequences of gambling before they ever place a bet.
