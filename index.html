1) Project Description

What it is & Why it exists

Odds Against Life is an immersive digital comic book designed to educate and warn about the devastating effects of casino addiction.

The Problem: Over 60 million people suffer from gambling addiction, which ruins families and health.

The Solution & Uniqueness

How it works: The comic creates an emotional deterrent. By showing the raw reality of addiction, it helps people stay independent before they ever place a bet.

Unique Feature: Unlike clinical brochures, we use engaging visual storytelling to reach our audience.
