1) Project Description

What it is & Why it exists

Odds Against Life is an educational digital comic designed to prevent gambling addiction[cite: 14, 15]. It targets people of all ages who are at risk of casino-related harm[cite: 16].

The Problem: 60 million people suffer from this addiction, which often leads to death or ruin[cite: 23].

The Solution & Uniqueness

How it solves the problem: The comic acts as a "visual shield." By showing the emotional and financial decay caused by gambling, it creates a psychological deterrent before the addiction starts[cite: 17, 18].

Unique Feature: Unlike dry statistics, our comic uses empathy and storytelling to make the danger "feel" real[cite: 25].
