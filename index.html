Try Demo Scroll

1) Project Description

The Concept

Odds Against Life is an interactive digital comic designed to prevent gambling addiction (ludomania) among youth.

We use visual storytelling to show the emotional and financial destruction caused by casinos, making the message more relatable than traditional lectures.

The Solution

By using a 16-page narrative arc, we lead the reader through a character's journey, ending with a powerful visual contrast between a life of addiction and a life of recovery.

2) & 4) Functionality & Tech Specs

Step-by-Step Experience

  • Vertical Scroll: Seamless transition between 16 A5-sized panels.
  • Narrative Flow: Story progression without complex buttons to maintain immersion.
  • Dual Ending: A final contrast screen showing parallel realities.

Tech Stack: HTML5, CSS3 for the web wrapper, and Digital Illustration for the A5 comic panels.

5) Sponsor Interview

Interview with Almas (Teacher)

Students: Is gambling a serious issue?

Sponsor: "Yes. Government bans on ads and strict age limits show it's a national problem affecting social stability."

Students: Should we focus on emotions or facts?

Sponsor: "Emotions. If readers feel the character's stress and regret, the message will be much stronger than just using statistics."

Students: What makes a powerful ending?

Sponsor: "A turning point. An ending that gives hope and shows the character choosing a better path is very meaningful."

Key Learnings

Based on this feedback, we made three major changes:

  1. Focus on Faces: Added more close-up emotional shots to the 16 pages.
  2. Refined Ending: Made the "Good Ending" about family reconnection, not just money.
  3. A5 Strategy: Confirmed that vertical A5 panels are the best for mobile readability.
