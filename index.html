1) Mission & Motivation

Our "Why"

Our motivation is to showcase **high-level teamwork** and **artistic mastery** while tackling a critical social issue. We aim to reach our peers through the visual power of Manga, helping them understand and avoid the trap of gambling addiction.

The Solution

By using an interactive 16-page narrative, we provide a "mental vaccine". We don't just lecture; we show the emotional and psychological consequences of every choice made by the protagonist.

2) Full Functionality

Interactive Webtoon Reader

The core functionality of our project is the custom-built vertical scrolling interface. Instead of a static PDF, we created an interactive digital environment.

  • Seamless Vertical Scroll: Mimics natural social media behavior, keeping teens engaged.
  • Live Progress Tracking: A dynamic red progress bar at the top visually indicates how deep the user is into the story.
  • Intuitive UX: No complex menus; the user simply scrolls to experience the narrative flow.

3) Final Sketches & Art Prototype

Visual Identity

We chose a Traditional B&W Manga Style. The absence of color intentionally strips away the "glamour" of casinos, forcing the reader to focus on the raw psychological distress and facial expressions.

The 16-Page Arc

The prototype consists of 16+ carefully storyboarded panels. The pacing is designed to start slow (the illusion of winning) and rapidly accelerate into chaos (the addiction trap).

Dual-Ending Concept

The final sketches reveal a Bifurcation Point. The 16th panel splits the narrative into two visual realities, empowering the reader to reflect on the consequences of their choices.

4) Technical Requirements

Frontend Architecture

The project is built using pure, lightweight web technologies to ensure maximum accessibility across all devices without requiring app downloads.

  • Semantic HTML5: For structured and accessible content delivery.
  • Custom CSS3: For responsive grid layouts, custom scrollbars, and interactive hover states.
  • Vanilla JavaScript: Implemented for real-time DOM manipulation (the scroll progress bar).

Cross-Platform Optimization

We adhered to strict technical constraints to ensure the comic loads instantly:

  • Responsive Design: Fluid containers that adapt from wide desktop monitors to narrow mobile screens.
  • Asset Scaling: A5 manga pages are digitally optimized via scaling algorithms to prevent pixelation.
  • Viewport Constraints: The custom `overflow-y` viewer protects the main page layout from breaking.

5) Stakeholder Interview

Insights from Almas (Project Sponsor)

Q: Why use Manga instead of a traditional poster?

Almas: "Engagement is key. A 16-page arc allows the audience to live through the character's stress. It makes the warning personal rather than theoretical."

Q: Is the Black & White style effective for this topic?

Almas: "Yes. It removes the 'false glamour' of gambling and forces the reader to focus on the grim psychological reality."

Q: What is the most important lesson for the reader?

Almas: "The realization of choice. Your parallel endings show that recovery is possible, but the 'trap' is devastating."

Key Adjustments

Based on our interview, we prioritized:

  • Emotional Close-ups: To build empathy.
  • Heavy Inking: To create a "suffocating" atmosphere in the addiction scenes.
  • Mobile Optimization: For instant accessibility.
