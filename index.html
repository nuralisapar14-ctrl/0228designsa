1) Project Description

What our product is

We are creating a high-quality, immersive digital comic book that tells the raw and emotional story of a person falling into, and fighting, casino addiction.

What problem it solves & How it works

The Problem: Over 60 million people worldwide suffer from gambling addiction, which ruins families and leads to severe debt or loss of life.

The Solution: The comic acts as a preventative psychological tool. By visualizing the devastating consequences *before* a person places their first bet, we create an emotional deterrent. It solves the problem through empathy and education rather than boring statistics.

Why this problem is important

Gambling addiction is a silent killer. People often don't realize they are trapped until it's too late. Intervening early with relatable media can literally save lives.

What makes our idea unique

Unlike clinical brochures or generic warnings, our project uses engaging visual storytelling. We speak the language of gamers and youth, turning a serious psychological warning into a captivating artistic journey.

2) & 3) Requirements

Functional Requirements

  • Vertical Scrolling: Users can smoothly scroll top-to-bottom to read the comic chapters.
  • Interactive Zoom: Readers can click or tap to zoom in on specific artwork details.
  • Responsive Design: The comic viewer adapts perfectly to mobile phones and desktop screens.

Technical Requirements

  • Programming Languages: HTML5 and CSS3 (No JavaScript required for base layout).
  • Version Control: Hosted on a public GitHub repository.
  • Hosting Platform: Deployed live using GitHub Pages.
  • Design Tools: Digital drawing software for sketches.
